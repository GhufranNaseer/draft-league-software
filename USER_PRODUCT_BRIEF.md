# User Product Brief

## Product
Participant Management and Live Draft System

## Purpose
This platform helps leagues manage participant registration, approval, and live team drafting in one place.

## Who Uses It
- Participants (players)
- Team managers
- League administrators

## What Users Can Do

### Participants
- Submit registration details and required files
- Track submission status
- Access personal profile after login

### Team Managers
- Open team dashboard
- View active round, timer, and eligible players
- Pick players during team turn
- Review recent draft activity

### Administrators
- Review, approve, reject, and edit participant submissions
- Manage categories, teams, and users
- Configure league setup and draft rounds
- Start, tick, and close draft rounds
- Export participant data

## Typical User Flow
1. Participant submits form.
2. Admin reviews and approves submission.
3. Admin sets up league rounds and teams.
4. Admin starts draft round.
5. Team managers pick players during timed turns.
6. Draft board updates in real time for all users.

## What Makes It Easy to Operate
- Clear role-based dashboards
- Simple step-by-step draft flow
- Real-time turn changes and activity feed
- Structured status transitions from registration to drafted

## Operational Guidelines
- Approve participant records before starting draft rounds.
- Confirm league type and round settings before draft start.
- Monitor active turn timers to reduce delays.
- Use activity and status views to verify actions.

## Success Criteria
- Faster participant processing
- Fewer manual draft errors
- Clear ownership of actions by role
- Smooth, transparent round completion
